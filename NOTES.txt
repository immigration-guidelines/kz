Kazakhstan - published administrative guidance on immigration case handling
Checked 2026-11-15.

Result: nothing obtained, and nothing that is guidance was found. Procedure is set by legal
acts: government resolutions (entry and stay of immigrants and migration control, No 148 of
2012; refugee status, No 183 of 2010), orders of the Minister of Internal Affairs (refugee
applications, No 496 of 2010; state-service rules) and of the Minister of Labour (work
permits for employers, No 559 of 2016; kandas status, No 329-Ө-М of 2013). The Migration
Service Committee of the Ministry of Internal Affairs and the Ministry of Labour publish no
methodological recommendations or instructions on deciding foreigners' cases: a title search
of their document lists finds methodological recommendations only on probation, traffic
inspectors, social work and labour norms.

The procedural acts could not be fetched under the rules of this collection:
- https://adilet.zan.kz (official legal database). robots.txt (read 2026-11-15) allows "*"
  except /api/, /search and service pages, but has a separate group for "GPTBot, ClaudeBot,
  anthropic-ai, Claude-Web, ..." with "Disallow: /". That rule names this client's family of
  agents, so the whole host was treated as closed. Archive copies were not used either.
- https://www.gov.kz (portal of all state bodies). robots.txt disallows /uploads/, /legalacts/
  and /page/ for all agents. Document pages (/org/<body>/documents/details/<id>) are allowed
  and were read, but they carry only the title and date; the text is an attachment under
  /uploads/. Most of the migration items there are drafts ("Проект НПА") and amending orders.
  Pages read: /org/qriim/documents (Ministry of Internal Affairs, incl. Migration Service
  Committee) filtered by title for "методическ", "мигра", "иностран", "беженц", "оказания
  государственн", "кандас"; /org/enbek/documents (Ministry of Labour) for "методическ",
  "иностранн", "кандас".
The main acts are listed in not-obtained.tsv with a gov.kz page a person can open.

How much real guidance: none is published. Kazakhstan regulates migration case handling
through registered legal acts (rules of state services, government resolutions), and no
methodological recommendation, instruction or circular for migration officers is public.
