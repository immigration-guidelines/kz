# Immigration guidance: Kazakhstan

Published guidance that the authorities of Kazakhstan issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in `NOTES.txt` and `not-obtained.tsv` here, and for all jurisdictions in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

**Nothing could be obtained for Kazakhstan.** The official legal database's robots.txt excludes AI crawlers by name, and the government portal disallows automated access to its attachments. No published guidance for officials was found. `not-obtained.tsv` lists the main procedural acts (on immigrants' entry and stay, refugee status, work permits and returnee status) with the page a person can open. Contributions are welcome.

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
